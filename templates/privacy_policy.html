Privacy Policy

Your business data, financial records, and operational metrics belong to you. Here is exactly how we handle, protect, and isolate your information.

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 1.2

1. Introduction

SmartBiz AI ("we", "our", or "us") provides retail management, inventory tracking, analytics, and artificial intelligence-assisted business recommendations. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and confidential business operations of every shop owner, manager, and staff member who uses our platform.

This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect when you access our web application, register an account, record sales or inventory, and how that information is used, stored, and protected.

2. Information We Collect

To provide high-accuracy analytics and operational tooling, SmartBiz AI collects information in the following categories:

  • Account & Profile Information: Full name, email address, phone number, encrypted password credentials, business role (Owner, Manager, Staff), and business profile details.
  • Business & Transaction Data: Product inventories, stock levels, wholesale and retail pricing, sales records, customer lists, and operating expense entries created through your business workspace.
  • AI Queries & Insights Data: Prompts submitted to the SmartBiz AI assistant, generated performance summaries, inventory forecast inquiries, and financial health reports.
  • Technical & Session Data: IP addresses, browser types, session timestamps, device identifiers, and login activity logs used for security auditing and account protection.

🔒 Strict Data Isolation: Your business records, revenue figures, and inventory details are kept strictly segregated by business account. We never share or sell your business numbers to competitors or third-party marketers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to operate and enhance your retail management experience:

  • Providing real-time analytics dashboards, profit margin calculations, and inventory tracking.
  • Triggering automated proactive alerts (e.g., low stock warnings, reorder suggestions, unusual expenses).
  • Generating contextual AI insights and plain-language summaries specific to your business performance.
  • Authenticating user sessions, preventing unauthorized access, and maintaining audit logs of critical updates.
  • Sending essential transactional messages, including verification emails, security notifications, and password resets.

4. Artificial Intelligence & Proprietary Business Data

SmartBiz AI integrates machine learning and intelligent models to analyze your business trends and provide recommendations.

  • No Model Training On Private Data: Your proprietary sales, profit margins, and inventory quantities are not used to train public or external AI models.
  • Isolated Inference: When you request AI analysis, only the relevant numerical summaries and prompts are securely processed in isolated execution contexts.
  • Advisory Nature: AI-generated forecasts and suggestions are supplementary tools intended to assist your operational judgment.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to ensure the confidentiality and integrity of your data:

  • Encryption: All data in transit between your browser and our servers is secured using modern TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Password Security: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using standard PBKDF2/SHA-256 algorithms and are never stored in plaintext.
  • Role-Based Access Controls: Granular permissions ensure that only authorized personnel within your business (Owner, Manager, Staff) have access to sensitive financial metrics.
  • Session Management: Active login sessions and security tokens are tracked with automatic expiration and deactivation capabilities.

6. Data Ownership & User Rights

You retain full ownership of all data you upload or generate in SmartBiz AI. You have the right to:

  • Access, review, and modify your personal profile and business operational details at any time.
  • Export your sales reports, customer logs, and inventory data in standard formats (CSV, PDF).
  • Request complete deletion or deactivation of your account and associated database records upon closing your business account.

7. Cookies & Session Storage

SmartBiz AI utilizes secure, HTTP-only session cookies and CSRF tokens strictly required for system functionality, user authentication, and CSRF protection. We do not use invasive third-party cross-site advertising trackers.

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact our support team at:
